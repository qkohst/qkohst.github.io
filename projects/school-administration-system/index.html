School Management Information System

An integrated school administration platform covering academics, student affairs, finance, and correspondence across six user roles.

1 / 7

A school management information system that consolidates administrative work normally scattered across files and spreadsheets. One platform handles school master data, student affairs, both student and school finances, and correspondence — with access rights separated according to each staff member's role.

There are six user roles: Admin, Correspondence Admin, Finance Admin, Teacher, Counselling Teacher, and Student. Each role sees only the menus and data it is responsible for, so a teacher simply records attendance for their own class, finance staff never touch academic records, and students only see their own information.

The system has been developed incrementally since 2021 and is still being extended — growing from a simple correspondence tool into a complete school administration platform, with WhatsApp notifications to parents and an interactive in-app guide.

Key Features

  • Six user roles with separated permissions: Admin, Correspondence Admin, Finance Admin, Teacher, Counselling Teacher, and Student.
  • School master data: school profile, academic year, teachers, staff, study groups, students, alumni, and departed students.
  • Student affairs: subjects, class timetables, and daily per-subject attendance recorded by the teaching staff.
  • Violation point system with automatic accumulation, recorded by the counselling teacher.
  • Digital correspondence: classification, incoming and outgoing mail, disposition, and mail agenda.
  • Student finances: billing details, payment transactions, and savings (deposits and withdrawals).
  • School finances: categories plus income and expenditure records.
  • Financial, attendance, and violation reports exportable to both Excel and PDF.
  • Automatic WhatsApp notifications to parents for attendance, violations, and transaction receipts.
  • In-app guidance: interactive tour, documentation, and contextual help on each page.
  • Bulk data import from Excel files and quick search on every data table.
