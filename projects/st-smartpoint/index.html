St SmartPoint

A web-based point-of-sale application for shops and minimarkets: sales, stock, supplier purchasing, and reporting in one system.

1 / 8

St SmartPoint is a web-based point-of-sale application for grocery shops, minimarkets, and small businesses that need a checkout system that is simple yet complete. Because it runs in the browser, it can be used from any till computer on the shop network without installing extra software.

The checkout screen is built for speed: products are found by typing a name or scanning an SKU, subtotals and change are calculated as you go, and the whole flow can be driven from the keyboard without touching a mouse. A single product can be sold in different units — a pack or a carton, for example — with stock still counted correctly.

The admin side handles master data, records supplier purchases that automatically increase stock, and produces daily, per-product, and per-cashier sales reports that export to Excel. The dashboard shows the sales trend, best-selling products, and low-stock warnings.

Key Features

  • Two user roles with separated permissions: Admin and Cashier.
  • Checkout screen with fast product lookup by name or SKU scan.
  • Multi-unit selling: one product can be sold in different units with automatic stock conversion.
  • Live calculation of subtotal, per-item discount, tax, total due, and change.
  • Quick cash-amount buttons and an exact-amount option to speed up payment.
  • Keyboard shortcuts for search, pay, and cancel.
  • Receipt printing to 58mm or 80mm thermal printers, or to A4 paper, per settings.
  • Cashier transaction history with receipt reprinting.
  • Master data: users, categories, units, products with multi-unit conversion, and suppliers.
  • Supplier purchase records that automatically increase product stock.
  • Daily, per-product, and per-cashier sales reports with Excel export.
  • Dashboard with sales trend, best-selling products, and critical stock alerts.
